How It Works

How os.neu works

Three simple steps from this page to a first screen

01

Download the kit

Save the boot kit from os.neuavenue.com/downloads/.

02

Unpack and burn

On Linux list disks, then burn only an empty stick. Never choose the disk that holds /.

03

Power on and speak

Choose USB in firmware and land on the NeuAvenue logo, then the AI prompt.

PC that already has Windows

The disk is not wiped. The next login opens the OS.neu graphical screen.

  1. Download the boot kit.
  2. Choose PC that already has an OS.
  3. When the bar hits 100%, hardware check → talk screen or DOS console.

No OS, or USB-only

Put OS.neu on a stick, then boot another laptop from USB.

  1. Download the kit or ISO.
  2. Choose USB without an OS.
  3. On the target PC press F8 (ASUS) and choose UEFI USB. If you see grub>, follow the USB tutorial.
